Methodology & sources

The signal is a weighted blend of five factor scores, each in −100 (wait) … +100 (sell here), computed for four windows (now, spring 2027, H2 2027, spring 2028):

  • Price trajectory (30%) — projected sale value plus momentum.
  • Financing & ERC (25%) — early-repayment charge while fixed, plus extra interest from holding past March 2027 at a higher remortgage rate.
  • Net proceeds (20%) — your personal cash-in-hand after all costs.
  • Seasonality (10%) — buyer-demand strength in the window's month.
  • Policy & macro (15%) — time-aware tally of the political factors.

The sell-vs-let comparison models monthly rental cash flow, income tax under Section 24 with the April-2027 landlord rate rise, and CGT with partial Private Residence Relief, then compares against selling now and investing the proceeds.

All figures are curated snapshots gathered 30 June 2026 and forward-looking estimates; they will go stale. Full sources are linked on the Market & factors tab.

Not advice. An informational model, not financial, tax, mortgage or legal advice. Forecasts are uncertain and often wrong. Verify against the linked sources and a qualified adviser before acting.
