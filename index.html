6 · Methodology & sources

The signal is a weighted blend of five factor scores, each in the range −100 (wait) to +100 (sell here), computed for four windows (now, spring 2027, H2 2027, spring 2028):

  • Price trajectory (30%) — projected sale value across windows plus price momentum.
  • Financing & ERC (25%) — early-repayment charge while in the fix, plus the extra interest cost of holding past March 2027 at a higher remortgage rate.
  • Net proceeds (20%) — your personal cash-in-hand after all costs.
  • Seasonality (10%) — buyer-demand strength in the window's month.
  • Policy & macro (15%) — time-aware tally of the political factors above.

All figures are curated snapshots gathered on 30 June 2026 and forward-looking estimates; they will go stale. The Bank of England base rate badge attempts a live refresh via a Cloudflare Function. Full source list is linked inline in each factor above.
