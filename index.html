Methodology & sources

Local market data (sales, listings, days-on-market, sold-vs-asking, £/m², rents and the new-build pipeline) is filtered to a strict 1 km radius of the N1 7TX centroid, from live Homedata, HM Land Registry, the EPC register, ONS and PlanIt — with a curated fallback where a live source isn't available.

Figures are a mix of live pulls and forward-looking estimates; some will go stale. Full sources are linked throughout.

Not advice. An informational model, not financial, tax, mortgage or legal advice. Forecasts are uncertain and often wrong. Verify against the linked sources and a qualified adviser before acting.
