weight 25%

7 · Methodology & sources

The signal is a weighted blend of five factor scores, each in the range −100 (wait) to +100 (sell here), computed for four windows (now, spring 2027, H2 2027, spring 2028):

  • Price trajectory (30%) — projected sale value across windows plus price momentum.
  • Financing & ERC (25%) — early-repayment charge while in the fix, plus the extra interest cost of holding past March 2027 at a higher remortgage rate.
  • Net proceeds (20%) — your personal cash-in-hand after all costs.
  • Seasonality (10%) — buyer-demand strength in the window's month.
  • Policy & macro (15%) — time-aware tally of the political factors above.

The sell-vs-let comparison (section 5) is separate from the signal: it models monthly rental cash flow (rent less voids, agent, maintenance, insurance, service charge and the full mortgage payment), income tax under Section 24 (interest gets a 20% credit) with the April-2027 landlord rate rise, and Capital Gains Tax on eventual sale with partial Private Residence Relief, then compares the total against selling now and investing the proceeds at your chosen opportunity rate.

All figures are curated snapshots gathered on 30 June 2026 and forward-looking estimates; they will go stale.
